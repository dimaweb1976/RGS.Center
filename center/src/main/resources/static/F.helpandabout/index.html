About

Robotic Gamma-Ray Spectrometer

RGS is an automated laboratory system for unattended gamma-spectrometric measurement of sample batches.

It identifies and weighs samples, transfers them to the shielded HPGe detector, acquires spectra in GammaPro and stores measurement files and reports in the batch database.
